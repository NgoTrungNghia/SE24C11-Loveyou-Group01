# Quickstart: 007 AI Matching & Preferences

## Testing AI Matching

1. Log into Dashboard at `http://localhost:5173/dashboard`.
2. Toggle the switch in top right corner to `🤖 AI Match`.
3. Notice candidates are ordered by score badges (e.g. `🤖 92% ăn ý`).
4. Update search preferences in `OnboardingWizard` Step 3 or via `PUT /api/ai/preferences`.
5. Observe candidate deck instantly updates to reflect new age, distance, and gender filters.
